Memberships

They say it is lonely at the top. We believe it is populated by the special ones. The ones that are beyond the sands of time. The ones restless to summit higher Everests. The Indus Club is proud that its walls are privy to the newsmakers, leaders and legends, as they recount their journeys, their breakthroughs, their next move and so much more.

Strictly by invitation or member referral, The Indus Club has a range of membership categories for CXOs, Founders, Board Members, Renowned Professionals and Influencers, spanning 3, 5 and 10 years. We have also designed special plans for Overseas, Next-gen and Women Leaders.

01

Individual

Leaders and newsmakers that are directly invited by the Club or referred by existing members.

02

Corporate

The rights and benefits of Corporate Members are the same as Individual Members. Additionally, they can avail exclusive offers through limited corporate plans.

03

Overseas

For individuals who have permanent residence elsewhere, but frequently visit Mumbai for business or leisure.

04

Next Gen Leaders

For individuals under 35 years of age who have climbed the ladder of success as young leaders or newsmakers.

05

Women Leaders

Women who have trailblazed their way through the industry and deserve the finest.
