Memberships

They say it is lonely at the top. We believe it is populated by the special ones. The Indus Club is proud that its walls are privy to the newsmakers, leaders and legends, as they recount their journeys, their breakthroughs, their next move and so much more. By invitation or member referral, The Indus Club offers 3 to 10-year membership plans for CXOs, founders, board members, renowned professionals and influencers, with dedicated plans for overseas members, next-generation leaders and women leaders.

01

Individual

Leaders and newsmakers that are directly invited by the Club or referred by existing members.

02

Corporate

The rights and benefits of Corporate Membership mirror those of Individual Membership, with additional access to exclusive offers through select corporate plans. The membership is held in the company’s name, which may nominate individuals to enjoy its benefits.

03

Overseas

For individuals who are based overseas but frequently visit Mumbai for business or leisure.

04

Next Gen Leaders

For individuals under 35 who have established themselves as young leaders and newsmakers.

05

Women Leaders

Women who have trailblazed their way through their respective fields and made their mark at the highest levels.
